## Notable Facts

<!-- Seeded from games.json — verify accuracy before use in content -->

- First Tomb Raider game developed by Crystal Dynamics, who had previously made Legacy of Kain.
- Introduced the grapple as a traversal tool.
- Received strong critical acclaim, revitalizing the series after Angel of Darkness.
- The game connects Arthurian mythology with the mystery of Lara's mother.
- Radio-support characters Zip and Alister provide real-time commentary during expeditions.
- Sold over 2.5 million copies in its first month.
